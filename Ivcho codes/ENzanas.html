About Me

A little more information about me!

Denislav Mihalev

Professional Actor

I completed my secondary education at NUMSI "Hristina Morfova" with a specialization in Acting. In 2025, I graduated from NATFIZ "Krastyo Sarafov" with a degree in Acting for Dramatic Theatre, in the class of Prof. Plamen Markov. I currently work as an actor at the Stefan Kirov Drama Theatre in Sliven. I have participated in numerous theatrical productions, including diploma performances at NATFIZ, productions at the Youth Theatre Studio "Scene 99," and musicals at the State Opera Stara Zagora. In addition to my acting career, I have a strong desire to develop as a professional photographer, combining my artistic experience with a creative vision in the field of visual arts.
