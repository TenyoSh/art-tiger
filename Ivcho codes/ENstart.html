Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services do you offer and what events are you suitable for?

We offer advertising photography for businesses, product campaigns and social media, birthday photography, corporate events, parties and other special occasions. We also provide animation services for children and interactive entertainment.

2. How can we book a photoshoot or an animator for an event?

You can contact us via the website contact form or directly by phone/email. After confirming the date, time, location and requirements, we finalize the booking with a contract and advance payment.

3. How long does photo editing take?

Event and birthday photos are usually delivered within 3–5 days, while advertising campaigns take up to 7 days, depending on the number and complexity of the shots.

4. Do you offer additional services such as decor, props or animation?

Yes, we can provide props, decorations and interactive games for children. For advertising projects, we can also suggest locations, models and concept-based shoots.

5. Can we see examples of your work?

Yes, our portfolio includes photos from advertising projects, events, birthdays and animation activities, showcasing our style and quality.
